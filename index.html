A whole nutrition clinic, in the browser.

Kindrdfood connects families with registered dietitians for video visits — and wraps the whole clinical workflow around it: scheduling, intake, charting, payments, and education.

What Kindrdfood is

A family signs up and builds a household profile — each member's conditions, allergies, intolerances, symptoms, and food preferences. They book and pay for a video consultation with a registered dietitian, complete a pre-appointment intake survey, meet the dietitian in a live in-browser video room, and afterward receive a clinical assessment plus recommended educational articles and dietitian-reviewed, allergen-safe recipes.

It isn't a directory or a booking widget bolted onto a calendar. It's a complete software clinic — scheduling, live video, clinical charting, billing, content, and audit — in one product.

Who it's for

Families

One account models a household — a head of family plus members, each with their own clinical profile and the ability to be the focus of a visit.

Dietitians

Providers set availability, run video visits, take session notes, write assessments, and recommend articles and recipes to each family.

Reviewers

QA dietitians tag recipes with allergens and move them through a multi-stage review before they're ever shown to a family.

Admins

Staff manage users, roles, scheduling, and analytics through an admin console and a custom role-based access manager.

Editors

Content authors write and publish the educational articles that dietitians attach to appointments as part of the care plan.

Book a dietitian in a few taps

A scheduling engine generates overlapping 30- and 60-minute slots from each dietitian's availability, handles buffers, and resolves conflicts automatically — booking a 60-minute visit gracefully frees up the overlapping 30-minute options.

  • Pay per visit, buy prepaid packages, or subscribe.
  • Saved-card checkout, coupons, and partner-comped sessions.
  • Automatic confirmation, 1-day, and 1-week reminders.

Built for real clinical care

Visits aren't just a video call. The intake survey threads answers forward into the dietitian's session notes and final assessment, so nothing gets re-asked. Pediatric growth charts track height and weight percentiles and z-scores against reference data, and food diaries capture what families actually eat between visits.

Every recipe a family sees has been tagged for allergens and signed off by a reviewing dietitian first.
