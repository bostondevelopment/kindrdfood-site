What Kindrdfood is

A physician refers a child into Kindrdfood, and the family is matched with a registered dietitian. The family builds a household profile — each member's conditions, allergies, intolerances, symptoms, and food preferences — then books and pays for a video consultation, completes a pre-appointment intake survey, meets the dietitian in a live in-browser video room, and afterward receives a clinical assessment plus recommended educational articles and dietitian-reviewed, allergen-safe recipes.

It isn't a directory or a booking widget bolted onto a calendar. It's a complete software clinic — scheduling, live video, clinical charting, billing, content, and audit — in one product.

Who it's for

Physicians

Referring doctors send a pediatric patient into Kindrdfood; the family is then matched with the right registered dietitian for their needs.

Families

The child patients and their parents. One account models the household — each child with their own clinical profile and the ability to be the focus of a visit.

Dietitians

Registered dietitians run the video visits, write assessments, author the educational content, and edit and allergen-review every recipe before a family sees it.

Behind the dietitians, an in-house chef developed the recipes, which the dietitians then edited and tagged for allergen safety — so everything a family saw had passed through clinical review.

Book a dietitian in a few taps

A scheduling engine generates overlapping 30- and 60-minute slots from each dietitian's availability, handles buffers, and resolves conflicts automatically — booking a 60-minute visit gracefully frees up the overlapping 30-minute options.

  • Pay per visit, buy prepaid packages, or subscribe.
  • Saved-card checkout, coupons, and partner-comped sessions.
  • Automatic confirmation, 1-day, and 1-week reminders.

Built for real clinical care

Visits aren't just a video call. The intake survey threads answers forward into the dietitian's session notes and final assessment, so nothing gets re-asked. Pediatric growth charts track height and weight percentiles and z-scores against reference data, and food diaries capture what families actually eat between visits.

Every recipe a family sees was developed by our in-house chef, then edited, tagged for allergens, and signed off by a dietitian first.
